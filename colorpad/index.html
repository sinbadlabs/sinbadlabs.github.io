Made for the moment

Coloring should feel like coloring—not managing software.

Children open a coloring app because they want to create. They should not have to work through complicated menus or constantly adjust settings.

Happy ColorPad stays clear and approachable, so the interface can fade into the background and creativity can stay in the foreground.

Everything they need

  • Color the pictures you already love

    Bring your own line art into Happy ColorPad directly from Photos. Drawings, printable coloring pages, and other line art can become part of your library.

  • 190+ pages ready to color

    Explore hand-drawn pages across animals, dinosaurs, fantasy, letters, numbers, holidays, and more.

  • Creative tools that feel fun to use

    Color with crayons, markers, watercolor, glitter, fill tools, and an easy-to-reach palette without losing focus on the picture.

  • A simple interface designed for touch

    Large, familiar controls make it easy to start and keep coloring on iPhone, iPad, and shared family devices.

  • No ads in the way

    Happy ColorPad avoids advertising, so children are not pulled away from creating by promotional interruptions.

  • A private gallery that stays local

    Projects and finished pictures stay on the device in a private local gallery, ready to revisit anytime.
