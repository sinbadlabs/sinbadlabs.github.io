Happy ColorPad

Bring your own pictures. Make them yours.

A simple, relaxing coloring app for iPhone and iPad. Choose from 190+ hand-drawn coloring pages, or import your own line art from Photos.

Download on the App Store

No ads · 190+ coloring pages · Import from Photos

Coloring should not feel like managing software.

Children open a coloring app because they want to create. They should not have to work through complicated menus, switch between collections, or constantly adjust settings before they can continue.

Happy ColorPad keeps the experience clear and approachable so the app can stay in the background and creativity can remain in the foreground.

Made to reduce interruptions

  • Color the pictures you already love

    Bring your own line art into Happy ColorPad directly from Photos. Drawings, printable coloring pages, and other line art can become part of your library.

  • 190+ pages ready to color

    Explore hand-drawn pages across animals, dinosaurs, fantasy, letters, numbers, holidays, and more.

  • Creative tools that feel fun to use

    Color with crayons, markers, watercolor, glitter, fill tools, and an easy-to-reach palette without losing focus on the picture.

  • A simple interface designed for touch

    Large, familiar controls make it easy to start and keep coloring on iPhone, iPad, and shared family devices.

  • No ads in the way

    Happy ColorPad avoids advertising, so children are not pulled away from creating by promotional interruptions.

  • A private gallery that stays local

    Projects and finished pictures stay on the device in a private local gallery, ready to revisit anytime.

Creative screen time for children and parents

Happy ColorPad gives children an approachable place to create while giving parents a quieter alternative to fast, high-stimulation entertainment.

Parents can also bring in line art their child already loves, turning familiar characters, interests, or family-made drawings into new coloring pages.
