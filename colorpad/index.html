Made for the moment

Coloring should feel like coloring—not managing software.

Open a page and the tools are already where you need them. There are no complicated menus to work through or settings to adjust before you begin.

Crayons, markers, colors, and fill tools stay close at hand while the picture remains the focus.

Good to know

Color independently—or together.

Choose from the built-in pages or add line art from Photos. Projects stay in the gallery between sessions, ready whenever you want to come back.

Can I color my own pictures?

Yes. Import line art directly from Photos, then keep it alongside the built-in coloring pages.

What comes with the app?

Explore 190+ hand-drawn pages and use crayons, markers, watercolor, glitter, fill tools, and color palettes.

Is Happy ColorPad free?

Happy ColorPad is free to download, with an optional in-app purchase for full access. Current details are shown on the App Store.

Does it show ads?

No. Happy ColorPad does not use advertising, so creative time is not interrupted by promotional content.

Where is artwork stored?

Projects and imported images stay locally on the device. Happy ColorPad does not upload artwork to Sinbad Labs servers.

Which devices are supported?

Happy ColorPad is available for iPhone and iPad and requires iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 or later.
