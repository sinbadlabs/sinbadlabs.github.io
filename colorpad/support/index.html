Frequently asked questions

Artwork and importing

Where are my projects stored?

Projects, imported images, and saved artwork are stored locally on your device. Happy ColorPad does not upload them to Sinbad Labs servers.

Will deleting the app remove my artwork?

Yes. Deleting Happy ColorPad or removing its app data may permanently remove your projects, imported pages, and saved progress. Export any artwork you want to keep before deleting the app or changing devices.

How do I import a coloring page?

Use the import option in Happy ColorPad to choose line art from your photo library. If access is unavailable, open your device's Settings and review Happy ColorPad's photo permissions. Follow the iPad import guide.

Subscriptions and purchases

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open the Settings app, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions to view, change, or cancel your Happy ColorPad subscription.

How do I restore a purchase?

Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase, then use Restore Purchases from Happy ColorPad's purchase screen. If it still does not appear, contact us by email.

Troubleshooting

Happy ColorPad is not working as expected. What should I try?

First, close and reopen Happy ColorPad and check that your device is running the latest available version of the app. If the issue continues, email us with these details. Avoid deleting the app unless you have exported the artwork you want to keep.

Contact Support

Email us at support@sinbadlabs.com. To help us investigate, please include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, Happy ColorPad version, and a short description of what happened.
