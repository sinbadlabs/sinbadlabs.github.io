About Sinbad Labs

Thoughtful software, made for real life.

Sinbad Labs is an independent app studio creating focused products that remove friction, respect attention, and make everyday moments feel better.

What we make

Our first product, Happy ColorPad, is a relaxing coloring app for families on iPhone and iPad. It combines 190+ hand-drawn pages with familiar creative tools and the freedom to import line art from Photos.

How we work

We design around the experience people came for. That means clear interactions, fewer interruptions, and careful decisions about what a product does not need.

Product development

Sinbad Labs also works with solo founders and small teams to clarify a product's core value, define a practical MVP, and build a focused first version.
